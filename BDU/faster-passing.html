How To Play The Ball Faster

A quick-play technique that enhances speed of play by receiving the ball with the back foot and immediately playing with the front foot. This efficient movement pattern allows players to maintain forward momentum, reduce touches, and execute faster transitions while maintaining control under pressure.

Punch Pass for faster play

The punch pass technique enables players to release the ball more quickly by reducing wind-up and striking through the ball with a compact motion.

Fittness follow the leader

Fitness and technical combination drill using sprint work around a confined area. Covers three variations: (1) speed dribble to short pass, (2) lead pass transitioning from a dribble, and (3) drop and go.
