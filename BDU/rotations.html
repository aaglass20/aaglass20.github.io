Rotations

U12 — 9v9 or Transition to 11v11 / 30-min halves

Development focus: Equal playing time, high energy, technical touches over endurance.

    Position Group Shift Length Why
    Wingers / Wide Mids 8 – 10 mins Small aerobic tanks; sprinting drops off quickly.
    Attacking Mid / Forwards 10 – 12 mins Keeps energy and high pressing aggressive.
    Defensive Mid / Fullbacks 12 – 15 mins Rotate at roughly the quarter mark.
    Center Backs / GK 15 – 30 mins Subbing halfway through the half or at halftime.

U13 – U14 — 11v11 / 35-min halves

Growth spurts & field size jump: Full pitch demands high stamina, but pacing skills are still developing.

    Position Group Shift Length Why
    Wide Midfielders (LM / RM) 10 – 14 mins Full 11v11 field creates heavy two-way running.
    Attacking Mid (OCM) / Forwards 12 – 15 mins Quick bursts exhaust players around minute 15.
    Defensive Mid (DCM) / Fullbacks 15 – 18 mins Often subbed once per half around the midway mark.
    Center Backs 35 mins (Full Half) Stabilizes the defensive line and offside trap.
    Goalkeeper 35 – 70 mins Half or full game.

U15 – U16 — 11v11 / 40-min halves

Tactical maturity: Players know how to pace runs, manage energy, and play through mild fatigue.

    Position Group Shift Length Why
    Wide Midfielders / Wingers 15 – 20 mins Fast-paced, but players have better aerobic bases.
    Attacking Mid / Forwards 18 – 22 mins Rotated once per half or adjusted based on game state.
    Central Midfield (DCM / CM) 20 – 40 mins Many coaches prefer leaving the central spine intact for full halves.
    Fullbacks 20 – 40 mins Full halves unless overlapping heavily.
    Center Backs / GK 40 – 80 mins Rarely subbed mid-half; mostly full halves or full games.

U17 – U19 / High School — 11v11 / 40–45-min halves

Competitive/College Prep focus: Longer minutes, tactical substitutions, and managing card/match tempo.

    Position Group Shift Length Why
    Wide Midfielders / Wingers 20 – 25 mins (or full halves) Rotated primarily for fresh legs against tired fullbacks.
    Forwards / #10s 20 – 30 mins (or full halves) Subbed tactically (e.g., chasing a goal, holding a lead).
    Defensive Mid / Fullbacks 40 – 45 mins (Full Half) Swapped mostly at halftime or late-game adjustments.
    Center Backs / GK Full 80 – 90 mins Almost never rotated unless injured, fatigued, or on a yellow card.

Key Takeaways Across Ages

    • The "Flank Rule" stays constant: From U12 to U19, wide players run the most distance at the highest speed, making them the most frequently rotated position on the pitch.
    • The "Spine" stabilizes with age: At U12/U13, rotating central players helps player development; by U16+, coaches leave the GK–CB–DCM spine intact to preserve tactical organization.
