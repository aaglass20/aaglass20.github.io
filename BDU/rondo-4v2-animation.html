Key Coaching Points

  • Max 2 touches — no dwelling on the ball; think ahead
  • Weight the pass to the receiver's front foot
  • First touch opens the body toward your next pass
  • Call it: "man on" means turn away; "turn" means space behind
  • Rotate defenders every 90 seconds — everyone experiences pressure
