Small-Sided Games

Capture the Flag warmup with bals

Six balls placed in the center, four bases, four players. Each player dribbles to collect as many balls as possible at their base. Players are allowed to steal from each other.

Hungry Hippos Relay

A fast-paced, competitive soccer drill that mimics the excitement of Hungry Hippos while developing essential soccer skills.

Sharks and Minnows

Fun game where one player (the shark) tries to tag the other players (the minnows) as they dribble across or around a designated area.

Sharks and Minnows with Passing

Three players start as sharks; four players start as minnows. Minnows dribble and pass to avoid losing possession to the sharks. If a minnow loses their ball, they become a stationary passing option for the remaining minnows.

Tic Tac Toe

Players dribble through a slalom pattern and place their pinnie in a tic-tac-toe grid. The first player to get three in a row wins.

Break the loc

Three rows of four players each. The top row starts with the ball and passes horizontally until they find a gap in the defensive line, then plays through to the back grid. The same sequence repeats from the bottom row.

Around the World

Set up 5 to 10 cones in a semi-circle around the goal box. Take a shot from each cone in sequence. If you miss, start over. The goal is to score from every cone without missing.
