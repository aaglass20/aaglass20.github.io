IGS Takeaways

Long Passes 4 People

4 people per group, 2 across from each other. Work on long passes, but trap, then pass to teamate next to you, then they send a driven pass. Next do same movement of trap and short pass but work long lofting pass or cross or chip. Next lets do a double pass or give and go. Should be working on communication inbound and outbound.

4 Person Movement Warmup

4 People constantly moving for 1-2 min. Try not to just move in a circle, should be constantly moving. Start with 2 touch, then 1 touch, 1 bounce juggle, no bounch juggle. Could evolve to headers, chest to thigh to foot. Goal is to keep moving, keep control

Closing Fast Shot Fast

The goal of ths drill is to work in closing on defence. Another goal is to shot fast. Put two goald about 20-25 yards out and devide team. Two goalies. Never leave anyone open. Close fast so they don't get a shot off. Offensive team should be shooting quick and often. Still use communication and movement tactics to better yourself. Great on the toes goalie traning.

Movement Skill Reset Find Target

Players run through the gate, call name to receive a pass. Then play it back and also saying their name. Then they must run through gate and find another person to receive a pass from. You can run this for 1 min or 2 and rotate players in middle with players on cone. You can run this for several skills. Two touch, volleys, headers, chest trap, thigh trap, foot trap, and Turns.

4 Point Shooting

4 Lines, rotate left to right. After each line shoots, for the next line they are in play to follow-up the shot. 1st line receives a short pass, turn, and quickly shoot. Second line receives pass from coach for 1 touch shot, player from line 1 is there for rebound. Third line makes a move, then shots. Player 1 and 2 are there for rebound. Line 4 puts up a cross. Player 1, 2, 3 are there for rebound. Repeat with next 4. Should have some pace to this. Good goalie work also.

Practice Layout

Structured templates for a typical 3-hour practice and a lighter 2-hour day-after-game session.

    Typical Practice (~3 Hours)

    1. Warmup Running — Touchline to sideline to 10–15 yards out (e.g., dynamic runs along the lines)
    2. Warmup Passing Drill — Foot skills, 4-person movement and touches
    3. Stretch
    4. Skill Drill — 1v1 or 2v2 long touches: two-touch driven passes, chips, or 2-person give-and-gos
    5. Fitness — Ladders
    6. Lesson Drill — Focused on session theme (e.g., 4-point shooting, or defensive closing / short-field shoot and close)
    7. Fitness — 1-mile run (5 laps around the field)
    8. Crossing Drill — Develops drops, communication, and crossing technique
    9. Stretch

    Day After Game (~2 Hours)

    1. Warmup Running
    2. Stretch
    3. Movement Passing Drill
    4. Ladders
    5. Long Passing Drill
    6. Small-Sided Scrimmage — High touches, recovery pace
    7. Stretch

Circle Passing Warmup

Coach has 5 balls. He plays ball to player, the control it and pass in one direction. The next player controls it and passes to the next and sprints to that cone. Use communicaiton. Go Fast. Add balls as the machine works. Focus on fast touch then pass. Go the other direction. If it get wonky, stop and reset.
