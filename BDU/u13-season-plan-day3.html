Day 3: Off-Ball Movement & Pressing

Create space. Win it back. Control the tempo.

Drill 1: Movement Patterns — Checking & Third-Man Runs

15 min Groups of 5 Intermediate

Set up a pattern: A passes to B, B checks back to receive, plays to C. As B receives, D makes a third-man run behind the defensive line. Rotate positions. Teaches coordinated off-ball movement.

  • Third-man runner times run with B's touch — not before
  • B checks back at an angle — creates space behind
  • Runner calls 'yes!' to signal the run is on
  • Pattern must become automatic — no thinking

Drill 2: Pressing Triggers — Recognition Game

15 min 6v4 Intermediate

6 attackers build possession against 4 defenders. Defenders are not allowed to press until a trigger occurs: a bad first touch, a back pass to the GK, or a pass going square. When a trigger fires, all 4 press together immediately.

  • All 4 press on the trigger — not just the nearest player
  • Press with cover — don't leave the central lane open
  • Nearest player goes hard; second player cuts the passing lane
  • Back to defensive shape if press fails

Drill 3: 6v6 Pressing Game

15 min 6v6 Intermediate-Advanced

6v6 in a 40x30 grid. Defending team scores a point by winning the ball and making 3 consecutive passes. Attacking team scores by making 10 consecutive passes. Encourages structured pressing from the whole team.

  • Press as a team — leave nobody isolated
  • Nearest defender presses; others cut angles
  • Communication is the press trigger — 'Now!' or 'Press!'
  • If ball escapes press, reset to shape before pressing again

Drill 4: Full Game with Pressing Cue Words

20 min Full team All levels

Full game. Coach or captain calls 'PRESS!' and the whole team shifts into a high press immediately. Coach calls 'SHAPE!' to reset. Players learn to flip between modes. Switch the cue caller to build leadership.

  • React in under 2 seconds on the call
  • Press goes OFF if opponent plays through — don't chase
  • Use cue words in games — build the habit now
  • Reward the team that presses successfully with a bonus point

Key Coaching Points

  • Off-Ball = On Duty: If you don't have the ball, you're still working. Make a run or create space.
  • Third-Man Concept: The ball goes to B so C can run. Understand you might be the enabler, not the scorer.
  • Trigger Recognition: Press on bad touch, back pass, or square ball — not randomly. Random pressing is energy waste.
  • Collective Press: One player pressing alone = pointless. It must be simultaneous.
  • Reset: A failed press must be followed by immediate shape reset. Chasing after a failed press creates gaps.
