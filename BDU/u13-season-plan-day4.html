Day 4: Finishing & Aerial Control

Technical precision in front of goal and in the air

Drill 1: Volley Circuit

10 min Pairs All levels

Players pair up. Feeder tosses ball at various heights — chest, waist, knee. Striker volleys back to the feeder. Rotate after 8 strikes. Progress to half-volleys and driven volleys.

  • Plant foot points at target
  • Non-kicking leg provides balance
  • Strike through the center of the ball
  • Keep head steady — don't look at the goal until after contact
  • Follow through toward target

Drill 2: Cross & Finish — Near / Far Post Runs

15 min Groups of 5-6 Intermediate

Winger dribbles to the byline and crosses. One attacker attacks the near post, one attacks the far post. Third runner checks to the penalty spot for a cut-back. Rotate roles every 3 crosses.

  • Near-post runner attacks the near post hard — get to the ball first
  • Far-post runner holds run until cross leaves the foot
  • Penalty-spot runner stays central — adjust to where ball drops
  • Goalkeeper challenges: must decide to come or stay

Drill 3: Aerial Control — Chest, Thigh, Head

15 min Individual/Pairs Beginner-Intermediate

Feeder serves balls in the air. Receiver controls with chest (to feet), thigh (kill the bounce), or head (redirect to a target). Station rotation — 5 touches each then switch.

  • Chest: create a platform — lean back slightly to cushion
  • Thigh: cushion, don't knee
  • Head: neck firm, eyes open, forehead contact not top of head
  • After control — immediately set up next touch
  • Don't freeze after controlling — move

Drill 4: Finishing Scenarios Game

20 min Full group All levels

Set up 4 finishing stations around the box — cutback finish, 1v1 with keeper, volley from cross, header from corner delivery. Rotate in groups of 3. Keeper in goal the whole time — create match-realistic finishing pressure.

  • Composure — slow down your thought, fast with your feet
  • Pick a corner before the ball arrives
  • Attack the ball — don't wait for it to come to you
  • One-touch finish preferred when possible
  • Miss → reset → next rep — no dwelling

Key Coaching Points

  • Composure: The moment of finishing is not the time to think. Decide early and commit.
  • Body Shape: Open hips and plant foot determine where the ball goes. Fix the shape, fix the finish.
  • Aerial Timing: For headers and volleys — be early, not late. Let the ball come to you only on a chest trap.
  • Cross Delivery: Wingers — aim for the 6-yard box at near-post height. Let the striker redirect.
  • Repetition: Finishing is a skill built through volume. Each player should get 15+ attempts per session.
