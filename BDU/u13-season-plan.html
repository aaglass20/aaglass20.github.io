U13 Season Plan

About This Plan

This season plan transitions our U13 group into full 11v11 positional play. The theme is Positional Play and Tactical Identity — crisp passing, intentional movement, and a coherent team shape. We carry forward the defensive aggression built last season while introducing new players to those principles. Training split: 40% Technical | 60% Tactical.

Season Theme: Positional Play

"Move the Ball. Move Yourself."

"Soccer is a team sport played with individual excellence."

Width & Depth. Use the full pitch — stretch the opponent horizontally and vertically to create gaps.
Third-Man Runs. The player who passes is never done — move to create the next option.
Shape First. Before you receive, know where you're passing. Shape is collective, not individual.

Tactical Identity

"Know Your Role. Execute Together."

"A team without an identity is eleven individuals. An identity turns them into a unit."

Compact Without Ball. When we lose possession, the team presses as a unit within 3 seconds.
Expansive With Ball. When we have possession, we spread the play to open lanes.
Quick Transitions. The moments of transition — gain or loss of ball — are when games are won.

Defensive Aggression — Carried Forward

"New Season. Same Standards."

"The new players learn what we already know: you win the ball by wanting it more."

50/50 Mindset. Make every 50/50 a 100/0. Desire determines the outcome.
Press With Intent. Press is always the right call. Apply pressure, disrupt rhythm, force mistakes.
Individual Defending. Side-on stance, patience, body contact first — the fundamentals never change.
