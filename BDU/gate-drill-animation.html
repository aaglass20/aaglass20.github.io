Key Coaching Points

  • Scan before the ball arrives — know your gate before contact
  • Inside foot for touches across body; outside foot for redirects away from pass
  • Soft touch — don't kill the pace; keep it playable
  • Progress: feeder calls the gate as the ball leaves their foot — force faster reaction
