Day 2: 11v11 Shape & Compactness

Building a coherent team structure in and out of possession

Drill 1: Shape Walk-Through (No Ball)

10 min Full team All levels

Walk the team through the 4-3-3 (or 4-4-2 if preferred) shape on a full-sized pitch. No ball — just positioning. Point out the lines, the distances between lines, and how shape changes when ball moves side to side.

  • Lines stay compact — max 10 yards between defensive and midfield line
  • Full backs tuck in when ball is central
  • Wingers tuck in when defending
  • Center backs split wide when GK has ball

Drill 2: Shadow Play — Attacking Phase (11v0)

15 min Full team Intermediate

GK starts. Team builds through the thirds with no opposition. Coach directs from sideline, freezing play to adjust shape. Focus is on width, depth, and third-man runs being automatic.

  • Wide players hug the touchline when we're building
  • 9 makes a run to stretch the defensive line
  • 10 drops to link play — don't cluster in the middle
  • Switch play when first side is blocked

Drill 3: Defensive Block — Compactness Shape

15 min Full team Intermediate

Place 6 opposition players in a build-up shape. Defensive team must organize into a compact block. Coach freezes play to show good/bad compactness. Progress: opposition passes, block shifts together.

  • Flat block of 4 defenders stays together — no gaps
  • Midfield line stays 10 yards ahead of defensive line
  • Ball-far winger tucks in — protect the central lane
  • Block shifts as a unit, not as individuals

Drill 4: 11v11 Shape Scrimmage

20 min Full team All levels

Full 11v11 with shape coaching from the sideline. Coach pauses play when shape breaks down (too spread, too narrow, lines not shifting). Restart from where stopped. Emphasize transition moments.

  • Shout the transition: 'Press!' or 'Shape!' when possession changes
  • Don't chase the ball — cover the space
  • First 3 seconds after losing ball = most dangerous — press immediately
  • Win ball → look forward first

Key Coaching Points

  • Lines of 4: The block of 4 defenders must function as a unit. One drifts, the whole line is exposed.
  • Compactness: Between lines max 10 yards. Compact teams give opponents no space to play through.
  • Width vs. Compactness: With ball = wide; without ball = compact. Players must know which phase they're in.
  • Transition Trigger: The moment possession changes, every player must react — not watch.
  • Communication: Shape requires voice. Every player talks. Silence = disorganization.
