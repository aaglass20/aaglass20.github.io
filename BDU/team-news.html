🏆🔒 Back-to-Back Brilliance! BDU U13 Girls Dominate Possession in 2–0 Home Shutout Over Shaker Heights

Independence, OH  ·  September 20, 2026
BDU 2 – Shaker Heights 0

Sunday evening, the Blue Devil United (BDU) U13 Girls Soccer Team delivered another masterclass performance on their home turf. Controlling the tempo from the opening whistle to the final horn, BDU rolled to a commanding 2–0 victory over Shaker Heights — earning their second straight win in dominant fashion!

⚡ Lightning Strike Out of the Gate

Just like their previous outing, BDU wasted no time making their presence felt. Under three minutes in (2:58), Evie Peters sparked the offense with a sharp setup pass to Whitney Phillips. Phillips transitioned the ball seamlessly into space, dialed in her angle, and slotted a clinical, pinpoint finish past the keeper to put BDU up 1–0 before Shaker Heights could even settle in.

BDU's relentless pressure paid off once again late in the first half at 29:22. Claire Brandt showcased sheer grit, bodying up her defender to win a tough physical battle before sending a soaring cross into the danger zone. Charlie Merker met the cross with a rocket of a blast on frame — and when the initial shot deflected back into play, Merker stayed laser-focused, following up with a delicate, composed soft touch into the back of the net to extend the lead to 2–0!

🏆 BDU U13 Girls Dominate Revere 5–1 for First Win of the Season

Independence, OH  ·  September 14, 2026
BDU 5 – Revere 1

Blue Devil United U13 Girls put on an absolute masterclass on Sunday afternoon, charging to an electrifying 5–1 victory over Revere. Backed by relentless pressure, sharp passing, and rock-solid defense, BDU earned their first win of the season in commanding fashion.

⚡ Fast Start & Immediate Response

From the opening whistle, BDU came out flying. Just 99 seconds in (1:39), Charlie Merker launched a blistering strike from the edge of the 18-yard box. The Revere keeper couldn't corral the heavy shot, and Whitney Phillips showed pure striker's instinct — crashing the net on the ricochet to bury the opener.

⚡ First Half Goals

  • 1:39 — Whitney Phillips (rebound off Merker's shot)  1–0 BDU
  • 14:28 — Revere (defensive mishap)  1–1
  • 15:43 — Charlie Merker (pounced on loose ball in box)  2–1 BDU

Revere managed to capitalize on a rare defensive mishap at 14:28 to level at 1–1, but BDU answered with authority just over a minute later. Charlie Merker pounced on a loose ball in the box to retake the lead at 2–1. BDU's split-goalkeeping tandem of Claire Brandt and Isla Zdolshek kept Revere in check heading into the break.

🔥 Second-Half Fireworks

If the first half showed grit, the second half was pure team artistry.

🎯 Goal of the Season Candidate — 54:14

  • Claire Suchan triggered the buildup with a smart layoff to Isla Zdolshek
  • Zdolshek found a darting Claire Brandt, who beat her marker with flair
  • Brandt threaded a pass into Charlie Merker
  • Merker unselfishly slipped it across — Phillips calmly slotted home  3–1

Minutes later (58:07), the duo struck again. Phillips delivered a pinpoint ball from midfield into Merker's path. Charlie turned on the jets, outpaced the defense, and slotted a clinical strike to the right of the keeper — 4–1.

Just 43 seconds later (58:50), Claire Brandt put an exclamation point on the performance — intercepting Revere's kickoff, dancing through multiple defenders on a sensational solo run, and ripping a laser into the left side of the net. 5–1. Game over.

⚡ Second Half Scoring

  • 54:14 — Whitney Phillips (assist chain: Suchan → Zdolshek → Brandt → Merker)  3–1 BDU
  • 58:07 — Charlie Merker (assist: Phillips)  4–1 BDU
  • 58:50 — Claire Brandt (solo run off intercepted kickoff)  5–1 BDU

🔵 BDU Breaks Through with 5–3 Win Over Geauga

Independence, OH  ·  April 19, 2026
BDU 5 – Geauga 3
⚡ Fast Start Sets the Tone

Claire Brandt opened the scoring early, continuing her relentless attack. After narrowly missing on a couple of chances—including a shot off the post—Claire stayed aggressive and finished unassisted to give BDU a 1–0 lead.

Moments later, JoJo Rollins used her signature long throw to create another opportunity, placing a perfect ball into the box. Charlie Merker was ready, finishing cleanly to put BDU up 2–0.

Geauga responded with a goal to cut the lead to 2–1, but BDU kept pressing. Whitney Phillips, who was applying constant pressure all game, broke free on a breakaway and calmly finished to extend the lead to 3–1 at halftime.

🔥 Second Half Push

BDU wasted no time after the break. Early in the second half, Phillips again made her impact felt, finding Emery Osysko with a well-placed pass. Emery finished with confidence, pushing the lead to 4–1.

Geauga Federation fought back, capitalizing on a scramble in the box to make it 4–2, but BDU answered once again. On a great transition play, Emma Barwacz launched a strong punt that found Phillips streaking downfield. Whitney controlled it and buried her second goal of the day, extending the lead to 5–2.

Geauga added one final goal late, but BDU held firm as the final whistle blew—securing a 5–3 victory.

💪 Team Effort Leads the Way

This match was defined by teamwork and persistence in the attacking third. BDU consistently followed up opportunities, applied pressure, and created chances through effort and smart play. Defensively, the team held strong, anchored by Hannah Bechlem and Emma Barwacz, who helped organize the back line and limit high-quality chances.
