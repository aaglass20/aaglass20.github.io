Day 1: Passing & First Touch Direction

Crisp passes, intentional first touch — the foundation of positional play

Drill 1: Rondo 4v2

12 min Groups of 6 Intermediate

Four players maintain possession against two defenders in a 10x10 grid. Emphasis is on weight of pass and first-touch direction — always receive away from the defender.

  • Play quickly — no more than 2 touches
  • Weight the pass to the receiver's front foot
  • First touch opens body toward your next pass
  • Communication: call "man on" or "turn"
  • Rotate defenders every 90 seconds

Drill 2: First-Touch Gate Drill

15 min Pairs Beginner-Intermediate

Set up small gates (2 cones, 1 yard apart) at angles around a player. A feeder passes from various directions; the receiver must direct their first touch through a specific gate. Develops intentional touch direction.

  • Scan before the ball arrives — know which gate you're hitting
  • Use inside/outside of foot to redirect
  • Soft touch — don't kill the pace
  • Progress: feeder calls the gate just as they pass

Drill 3: Combination Passing Pattern (1-2 & Overlap)

15 min Groups of 4-5 Intermediate

Set up a passing pattern using cones: Player A passes to B, receives a 1-2 back, then lays off to C who overlaps. Rotate positions each round. Teaches automatic combination play.

  • Pass and immediately move — don't freeze
  • Overlap runner must time the run to arrive as the ball does
  • Weight the lay-off — it's not a shot
  • Call the overlap early so the runner knows to go

Drill 4: 2-Touch Possession Game (5v5+2)

20 min 5v5 + 2 neutrals Intermediate

5v5 in a 30x20 grid with two permanent neutral players always playing for the team in possession. Max 2 touches enforced. Team scores a point for 6 consecutive passes without losing possession.

  • Move into passing lanes before receiving
  • Neutrals play fast — reward them with good service
  • Lose ball → press immediately, don't stand
  • 2-touch rule forces quick thinking

Key Coaching Points

  • Weight of Pass: Too hard = uncontrollable. Too soft = intercepted. Find the pace that lets the receiver play quickly.
  • First-Touch Direction: The first touch is a pass decision. Know where you're going before the ball arrives.
  • Body Shape: Open your hips so your first touch opens you toward the center of the pitch.
  • Scanning: Eyes should scan once before the ball comes. Freeze = problem. Move = solution.
  • Communication: Quiet teams lose possession. "Man on", "turn", "give" must be automatic.
