Marking

4v4 Man Marking Drill

Small field, 4 players per team, 1 goalie per team. Each player is responsible for marking one opponent. The objective is to maintain tight marking while allowing the goalie to play the ball out. This drill emphasizes the importance of communication, positioning, and understanding of marking principles.

Pressing 2 V 3

Red (3 players) vs. Yellow (2 players). Yellow plays the ball out while Red swarms two defenders to the ball and the third Red player man-marks the remaining Yellow player. Yellow's objective is to pass into the small goal; Red presses toward the large goal.

You lose your man, you run

A 4v4 or 3v3 man-marking drill with the coach acting as the outlet or goalkeeper. If the player you are marking gets open, the coach plays the ball down field and both teams continue until you recover to your mark.

1 on 1 Rondo Marking

Two rectangular grids placed side by side. Three players from each team set up on the outside of their respective grids. Play starts on the right — a player attempts to get open while being marked. When they break free and receive a pass, the ball switches to the left grid and that player transitions over as the new marker.
