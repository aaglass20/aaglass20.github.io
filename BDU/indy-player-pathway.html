Stage 1 · Loving the Game

U8–U9 Ages 6–8

Discovery Phase
Game format4v4, no goalkeepers (7v7 at U9)
Sessions45–60 min · 1–2 a week

ThemeLove of the Game and a Ball at Every Foot

Training ratio 80% technical20% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • Dribbling with both feet
  • Turns: pull-back, inside cut, outside cut
  • Stopping and starting with the ball
  • Short passing and receiving with the inside of the foot
  • Striking a still ball at goal

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • Go to goal
  • Spread out — don't bunch around the ball
  • Attack one way, defend the other
  • 1v1 and 2v1

🏃 Physical

  • Balance and coordination
  • Running, jumping, skipping, tag games
  • Change of direction

🎯 Mental

  • Concentration in short bursts
  • Trying new things without fear of mistakes
  • Listening for one instruction at a time

🤝 Social

  • Fun with the ball and friends
  • Sharing and taking turns
  • Encouraging teammates

⚡ Attack Mentality

Be brave with the ball: every player dribbles at goal and shoots without fear of losing it.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Dribbles with either foot and looks up between touches
  • Turns away from pressure with at least two different turns
  • Passes to a teammate 5 yards away and controls the ball coming back
  • Can't wait for the next practice

Coaching this age

  • A ball for every player, and games instead of lines
  • Explain in 30 seconds, then let them play
  • Praise effort and bravery, not the score

Stage 2 · Skill Under Pressure

U10 Ages 8–9

Foundation Phase
Game format7v7
Sessions60 min · 2 a week

ThemeFundamentals, Fun, and Confidence with the Ball

Training ratio 70% technical30% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • Dribbling with all foot surfaces
  • Basic passing and receiving
  • Shooting technique
  • Shielding
  • Introduction to the weak foot

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • 1v1 play
  • Spacing
  • Basic positions
  • Triangle passing

🏃 Physical

  • Coordination and balance
  • Quick feet and agility
  • Movement fundamentals

🎯 Mental

  • Focus and effort
  • Discipline
  • Emotional control and a growth mindset

🤝 Social

  • Teamwork and empathy
  • Winning and losing gracefully

⚡ Attack Mentality

Develop an aggressive mindset through decisive movement, quick decisions and an explosive first step, staying composed under pressure on both sides of the ball. Always be aware of the chance to score, with the confidence to take calculated risks.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Beats a defender 1v1 with a move they chose
  • Receives across the body and passes with either foot over 10 yards
  • Shields the ball for 3 seconds under pressure
  • Knows the job of at least two positions

Coaching this age

  • Let players solve 1v1s on their own before stepping in
  • Rotate every player through every position, goalkeeper included
  • Ask questions ("where is the space?") more than you give answers

Stage 2 · Skill Under Pressure

U11 Ages 9–10

Expansion Phase
Game format9v9
Sessions75 min · 2 a week

ThemeSpeed of Play and Team Shape

Training ratio 60% technical40% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • First touch
  • Passing variety
  • Crossing
  • Defensive footwork
  • Weak-foot passing

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • Formations
  • Support angles
  • Pressure, cover, balance
  • Transition

🏃 Physical

  • Endurance, built through games
  • Agility and quickness
  • Acceleration over 5–10 yards

🎯 Mental

  • Confidence
  • Focus
  • Looking after their own gear and preparation

🤝 Social

  • Talking on the field: names, "man on", "time"
  • Picking a teammate up after a mistake
  • Learning to train and compete

⚡ Attack Mentality

Play forward first: look for the forward pass or dribble before the safe one, and be the first to react when the ball changes hands.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Takes a first touch away from pressure
  • Shows pressure, cover and balance in 2v2 and 3v3
  • Knows their role in the team's 9v9 formation
  • Reacts within 3 seconds when possession changes

Coaching this age

  • Teach the 9v9 shape through small-sided games, not lectures
  • Coach in the flow; stop the game only for the session's one big idea
  • Count transition moments out loud — "3 seconds to win it back"

Stage 2 · Skill Under Pressure

U12 Ages 10–11

Reinforcement Phase
Game format9v9
Sessions75–90 min · 2–3 a week

ThemeGame Understanding and Technical Maturity

Training ratio 50% technical50% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • One- and two-touch play
  • Volleys
  • Advanced dribbling
  • Long passes

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • Playing in lines
  • Zonal defending
  • Give-and-go
  • Switching play

🏃 Physical

  • Speed and agility
  • Core strength and body control through growth
  • Repeat sprints with the ball

🎯 Mental

  • Problem-solving on the field
  • Growth mindset
  • Preparing themselves for training and games

🤝 Social

  • Student of the game: watch, read and play
  • Giving and taking feedback from teammates
  • Competing hard and fairly

⚡ Attack Mentality

Combine to break lines: give-and-gos, third-player runs and switches of play to get behind the defense, then finish early.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Plays two-touch under pressure
  • Switches play with a 25-yard pass
  • Defends a zone as part of a unit
  • Can explain why a decision was right or wrong

Coaching this age

  • Set problems ("score after a switch") and let the group find the answer
  • Demand quality on the weaker foot in every exercise
  • Give players a voice: ask what they saw before telling them

Stage 3 · Team & Identity

U13 Ages 11–12

Transition to 11v11
Game format11v11
Sessions90 min · 3 a week

ThemePositional Play and Tactical Identity

Training ratio 40% technical60% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • Crisp passing
  • First-touch direction
  • Finishing
  • Aerial control

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • Full 11v11 shape
  • Off-ball movement
  • Pressing
  • Compactness
  • The offside line

🏃 Physical

  • Fitness for the full-size field
  • Speed, agility, quickness (SAQ)
  • Coordination through growth spurts
  • Introduction to bodyweight strength

🎯 Mental

  • Emotional control
  • Accountability
  • Patience with a bigger field and a new role

🤝 Social

  • Respect for coaches, referees and the game
  • Responsibility to their line (defense, midfield, attack)

⚡ Attack Mentality

Attack as a unit: time runs off the ball, arrive in the box in numbers, and press at once to win it back high.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Knows their role in the 11v11 shape with and without the ball
  • Times runs to stay onside
  • Presses on a trigger together with teammates
  • Controls a ball out of the air and plays it on

Coaching this age

  • Walk through the 11v11 shape in and out of possession before playing it
  • Keep small-sided games in every session — the big field hides players
  • Watch for growth spurts: adjust the load and expect a clumsy month

Stage 3 · Team & Identity

U14 Ages 12–13

Player Identity Phase
Game format11v11
Sessions90 min · 3 a week

ThemePlayer Specialization and Performance Goals

Training ratio 35% technical65% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • Precision
  • Using both feet
  • Position-specific skills

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • Advanced team tactics
  • Adaptive decisions
  • Fluid roles
  • The team's style of play
  • Set pieces

🏃 Physical

  • Strength training (bodyweight first, then supervised)
  • Power and explosion
  • Speed, agility, quickness (SAQ)
  • Nutrition

🎯 Mental

  • Leadership
  • Toughness
  • Setting and owning personal goals

🤝 Social

  • Winning mentality
  • Respect for coaches and the game
  • Leading by example in training

⚡ Attack Mentality

Know your weapon: every player builds a signature strength in the final third and uses it to decide games.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Plays a primary and a secondary position well
  • Uses both feet under pressure
  • Adjusts when the opponent changes shape
  • Has a personal development goal and tracks it

Coaching this age

  • Give each player a primary and a secondary position and train both
  • Set individual goals with each player and revisit them
  • Hand over parts of the session: captains run warmups, players call set pieces

Stage 4 · Playing the Game

U15 Ages 13–14

Competitive Performance Phase
Game format11v11
Sessions90 min · 3–4 a week

ThemeExecution, Maturity, and Game Impact

Training ratio 30% technical70% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • Execution at speed
  • Ball mastery
  • Tactical finishing

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • Systems of play
  • Mid-game adjustments
  • Leadership on the field
  • Game management

🏃 Physical

  • Strength, speed and power
  • Conditioning
  • Recovery habits: sleep, hydration, cool-down

🎯 Mental

  • Self-driven growth
  • Excelling under pressure
  • Honest self-review after games

🤝 Social

  • Team culture
  • Holding teammates to the standard
  • Mentoring younger players

⚡ Attack Mentality

Be ruthless and efficient: recognize the moment to speed up, create the high-percentage chance and finish it.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Executes technique at full game speed
  • Plays in more than one system
  • Recognizes and makes adjustments during a game
  • Takes care of their own recovery and preparation

Coaching this age

  • Train at game speed with game consequences
  • Use film or a whiteboard to review decisions, not just effort
  • Let players solve in-game problems before you call the change

Stage 4 · Playing the Game

U16–U18 Ages 14–18 · High school

Performance Phase
Game format11v11
Sessions90–120 min · 4–5 a week in season

ThemePlaying the Game: Excel Under Pressure and Win

Training ratio 25% technical75% tactical

⚽ Technical Focus

  • Execution at game speed under pressure
  • Mastery of their position
  • Set-piece delivery and finishing
  • A weak foot they can rely on

🧠 Tactical Concepts

  • Several systems of play
  • The team's game model
  • Plans for a specific opponent
  • Managing a game: protecting a lead, chasing a goal
  • Set pieces

🏃 Physical

  • Peak conditioning
  • Strength, speed, power
  • Comprehensive conditioning
  • Recovery, sleep and nutrition
  • Injury prevention

🎯 Mental

  • Excelling under pressure
  • Achieving winning results
  • Self-analysis from film

🤝 Social

  • Leading and mentoring younger BDU players
  • Representing the program
  • Planning their own path: school, club, college

⚡ Attack Mentality

Win the game: impose the team's style, manage momentum, and take the decisive action when it matters.

By the end of the season, a player…

  • Performs their role in the game model at speed
  • Stays composed and effective in big moments
  • Leads teammates on and off the field
  • Trains and recovers like an athlete

Coaching this age

  • Prepare for the opponent: each week has a game plan
  • Demand standards; let the captains enforce them
  • Manage the load across the week: hard, medium, light, game
