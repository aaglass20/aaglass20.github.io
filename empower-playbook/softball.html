Softball

Catching, fielding, hitting, and running — big smiles guaranteed

Program Overview

Two tiers with a scrimmage focus — every batter gets a tee and every player gets on base

🌱 Youngs & Intermediate

Warm-Up + Drills + Scrimmage

1 hr 15 min session. Warm-up followed by 1–2 drills, then a 35–40 minute scrimmage with coach-supported batting tee.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (10 min)
  • Drill 1 (10–15 min)
  • Drill 2 (10 min, some weeks)
  • Water Break (5 min)
  • Scrimmage (35–40 min)
  • Tee available for every batter
⭐ Experienced

Catching Drill + Scrimmage

1 hr 15 min session. Quick warm-up and partner catching drill, then maximum scrimmage time.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (5–10 min)
  • Partner Catching (10 min, most weeks)
  • Water Break (5 min)
  • Scrimmage (50–55 min)
  • Week 3: Full scrimmage session (55 min)

Drills Library

5 drills across 3 categories — fielding, hitting, and base running

⚾

Ground Ball

Players form a line in the infield. Coach rolls ground balls one at a time. A volunteer is positioned at first base. Each player fields the grounder and throws to first.

Build basic fielding mechanics — footwork, glove position, and throwing to a base.

Skill 🥎 Softballs 📍 Cones
☁️

Pop Flies

Players form a line in the outfield. Coach tosses a fly ball up in the air. Player tracks and catches it, then throws back to coach.

Develop the ability to track and catch balls hit in the air — a key confidence-builder.

Skill 🥎 Softballs 📍 Cones
🤝

Partner Catching

Partners play catch starting close together (5–8 ft), then gradually move apart as comfort increases. Focus on proper catching form — two hands, soft hands, track the ball.

Build throwing and catching distance gradually. The go-to warmup drill for every session.

Skill Team 🥎 Softballs
↔️

Partner Ground Balls

Partners face each other and take turns rolling ground balls across to the other. Work on getting in front of the ball and using proper fielding position.

Develop fielding footwork and glove-to-throwing-hand transfer with a partner.

Skill Team 🥎 Softballs
🎯

Tee Hitting

Tee is set up near the fence. Players take turns hitting the ball into the fence — one at a time with coach support. Coach teaches fundamentals: grip, stance, swing path.

Core hitting mechanics in a no-pressure environment. Every participant can make contact.

Skill 🥎 Softballs 🏏 Tee 🪵 Bat
💡 Let participants feel the contact first — adjust grip and form gradually once they've experienced success. Celebrate every solid hit!
🏃

Base Running

Players line up at home plate. Coaches and volunteers are stationed at each base to provide direction and encouragement. Players are sent one at a time around all four bases.

Learn the base paths and the feeling of scoring — everyone crosses home plate.

Skill Fun ⬡ Bases (4)
💡 Station a volunteer at each base who gives enthusiastic high-fives and encouragement. The round-trip around the bases should feel like a victory lap!

Scrimmage Format

All players take an at-bat before sides switch. Coaches are on the field at all times. A batting tee is available for every batter — no one sits down without swinging. Celebrate every hit and every out equally!
