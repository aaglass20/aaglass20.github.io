Football

Throwing, catching, and running — touchdowns for everyone

Program Overview

Two tiers built around big plays and bigger celebrations

🌱 Youngs

Full Drill Session

Full 75-minute drill session covering throwing, catching, agility, and games — with coach and volunteer support throughout.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (15 min)
  • Water Break (6 min)
  • Drill 1 (12 min)
  • Drill 2 (12 min)
  • Water Break (6 min)
  • Drill 3 (12 min)
  • Drill 4 / Game (12 min)
  • Total: 1 hr 15 min
⭐ Advanced

Drill + Scrimmage Session

One or two focused drills followed by a 40-minute scrimmage. Coaches serve as QB for both teams simultaneously.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (10 min)
  • Drill 1 (10 min)
  • Water Break (5 min)
  • Scrimmage (40 min)
  • 3–5 ES players + 1–2 peers/team
  • Coaches play QB and coach simultaneously

Drills Library

10 drills across 4 categories — throwing, catching, agility, and games

🎯

QB Throwing Nets

Players throw from marked spots at a target net. Start close, move back as the drill advances. Competitive version: two nets, teams race to hit the most targets.

Build QB throwing mechanics and grow confidence with distance in a no-failure format.

Skill Fun 🏈 Footballs 🥅 Throwing nets (2)
⭕

Circle Passing

Players sit or stand in a circle. Before each pass, the thrower calls out the name of the person they're throwing to, then delivers the ball.

Practice throwing accuracy and build communication skills within the group.

Team Skill 🏈 Footballs
🏃

WR Route Running

Cones and color spots set up as route markers from the 10-yard line to the goal line. Each player runs to a spot of their choice, coach throws as QB, player catches the ball and scores in the end zone — with a full celebration every time.

Introduce route concepts and catching while moving. The end zone celebration is the most important part.

Skill Fun Team 📍 Cones 🔵 Color spots 🏈 Footballs
💡 Let players pick their own route spot — ownership increases buy-in. The end zone celebration is the most important part!
🏃

Running Back Agility

Coach hands off the ball. Player runs through the agility ladder and over hurdles, then scores a touchdown in the end zone.

Develop ball security and agility skills while building toward one of the best moments in football — scoring.

Agility Fun 🪜 Agility ladders 🚧 Hurdles 🏈 Footballs
🌈

Color Spot Agility

Color spots are spread randomly around the field. Players hold footballs. Coach calls a color — all players sprint to the nearest spot of that color. First there wins the round.

Build reaction time, listening skills, and movement with the ball in hand.

Agility Fun 🔵 Color spots (20+) 🏈 Footballs
🚦

Red Light, Green Light

Players run with football from goal line to the opposite goal line. Freeze in place on "red light." First player to reach the far side wins.

Practice ball security while running and develop stop-and-start control in a competitive race.

Fun Game 🏈 Footballs
🦈

Sharks and Minnows

Players run from baseline to baseline carrying a football, avoiding the "shark" (coach). If tagged, that player becomes a shark and joins the coach in the middle.

Practice running with the ball and develop spatial awareness under light pressure. High energy, big laughs.

Fun Game 🏈 Footballs
🏟️

Scrimmage

Two teams compete in a standard play format. Coaches play QB for both teams. Every touchdown is celebrated with maximum energy from coaches and volunteers alike.

Apply all skills in a real game environment. Every score is a moment — make it one.

Game Team 🏈 Footballs 🟡 Pylons 🦺 Pinnies
