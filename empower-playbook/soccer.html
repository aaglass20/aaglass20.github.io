Soccer

Dribbling, passing, shooting — the beautiful game, adapted for everyone

Program Overview

Two tiers, one field — adapted for every ability level

🌱 Youngs & Intermediate

Full Drill Session

Players spend the full 75-minute session rotating through structured drills with coach and volunteer support at every station.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (15 min)
  • Water Break
  • Drill 1 (12 min)
  • Drill 2 (12 min)
  • Water Break
  • Drill 3 (12 min)
  • Drill 4 / Mini Game (12 min)

Focus on individual skills and fun mini-games throughout the session.

⭐ Advanced

Drill + Scrimmage Session

Experienced players run 1–2 focused drills then move into a 40-minute scrimmage with peer volunteers on the field.

  • Warm-up (10 min)
  • 1–2 Drills (10 min each)
  • Water Break
  • Scrimmage (40 min)

5–7 ES players + 1–2 peers per team. Coaches on field at all times.

Drills Library

All soccer drills organized by category — click a tab to explore

🔶

Dribbling Agility

Cones, ladders, and hurdles set up as an obstacle course. Players first go through without ball to learn the path, then dribble through at their own pace. BDU/volunteer walks alongside.

Purpose: Ball control at pace, agility, confidence with obstacles.

Agility Skill ⚽ soccer balls 🔶 cones 🪜 agility ladders ⛳ hurdles
💡 Let participants run through without a ball first — this removes the dual-task pressure and builds spatial confidence before adding ball control.
↔️

Passing Lines

Two facing lines with one ball. First player passes across to first player in opposite line, then goes to back of that line. Constant rotation and movement.

Purpose: Passing technique while moving; teamwork.

Skill Team ⚽ soccer ball 🔶 cones
🤝

Partner Passing

Partners across from each other passing back and forth. Coaches circulate and offer individual instruction. Start close, gradually add distance as confidence grows.

Purpose: Repetitive passing technique in a comfortable pairing.

Skill Team ⚽ soccer balls
⚡

Pass to Shot

Two lines in front of goal: one line has balls and passes to the other line; receiver takes a first touch and shoots.

Purpose: Combining receiving with finishing.

Skill Team ⚽ soccer balls
🏃

Dribble to Shot

Single line in front of goal with cones ahead. Each player dribbles through the cone obstacle then shoots on goal.

Purpose: Dribbling into finishing.

Skill ⚽ soccer balls 🔶 cones
🎯

Penalty Shooting

Players line up at penalty spot and take turns shooting. Coach or volunteer plays goalkeeper. Competitive version: two teams race to make the most goals in a set time.

Purpose: Shooting confidence and accuracy.

Skill Fun Game ⚽ soccer balls 🔶 cones
💡 Let the goalkeeper be dramatic about the saves they miss — falling dramatically when they "can't stop" a great shot is hilarious and empowering for the shooter!
🚧

Construction Zone

Cones and pylons spread randomly across field. Every player dribbles around knocking them over. Once all are down, reset and repeat. Coaches may reset during play to keep it continuous.

Purpose: Free-form dribbling in all directions with a playful goal.

Fun Agility ⚽ soccer balls 🔶 cones 🟠 pylons
📏

Longest Shot Competition

All players shoot from the same starting distance. After each round, the line moves back one step. Repeat until there's a winner.

Purpose: Shooting power and confidence from distance.

Fun Game ⚽ soccer balls 🥅 goal
🏟️

Scrimmage

5–7 ES players + 1–2 peer volunteers per team. Coaches on field with both teams. Standard play with coaches coaching alongside.

Purpose: Full game experience and team play.

Game Team ⚽ soccer balls

Scrimmage Format

5–7 ES players + 1–2 peer volunteers per team on the field. Coaches on field with both teams. Rotate positions to give everyone playing time in different roles. Every goal is celebrated by everyone!
