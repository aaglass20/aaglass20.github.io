Basketball

Dribbling, passing, shooting — built for every ability level

Program Overview

Two tiers built for different experience levels — drills-focused for newer players, scrimmage-heavy for experienced ones

🌱 Youngs & Intermediate

Drills-Only Session

Full 75-minute drill session with coach and volunteer support at every station.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (15 min)
  • Water Break (6 min)
  • Drill 1 (12 min)
  • Drill 2 (12 min)
  • Water Break (6 min)
  • Drill 3 (12 min)
  • Drill 4 / Game (12 min)
  • Total: 1 hr 15 min
⭐ Advanced

Drill + Scrimmage Session

Two focused drills followed by a 40-minute scrimmage with peer volunteers on the court.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (10 min)
  • Drill 1 (10 min)
  • Drill 2 (10 min)
  • Water Break (5 min)
  • Scrimmage (40 min)
  • 3–5 ES players + 1–2 peers/team
  • Coaches on court at all times

Drills Library

16 drills across 4 categories — dribbling, passing, shooting, and games

🏀

Individual Dribbling Practice

Ball slams, wrap around head/waist/knees, figure 8, both-hand dribbling, crossovers. All stationary and self-paced.

Learn the basics before introducing motion — build confidence with the ball in hand.

Skill 🏀 Basketball per player
🏁

Dribbling Relay Race

Teams of 5. First player dribbles to far cone and back, then passes gently to the next player. Friendly competition that teaches handling wins and losses.

Build dribbling under light pressure and introduce positive competition.

Team Skill 🏀 Basketballs 📍 Cones/Spots
🦛

Dribbling Hungry Hippos

Two teams on opposite baselines. Each player dribbles to half-court, grabs a color dot, and returns. Most dots collected wins.

Combine dribbling, spatial awareness, and friendly competition in one high-energy game.

Fun Team 🔵 20+ color dots 🏀 One basketball per player
🔶

Dribbling Obstacle Course

Navigate agility ladder and hurdles while dribbling or carrying the ball. Start with one foot per square, then increase complexity as skill grows.

Develop coordination, agility, and ball control simultaneously.

Agility Skill 🪜 Agility ladders 🚧 Hurdles 📍 Cones
📍

Stationary Partner Passing

Partners stand 10–15 ft apart on marked spots and pass back and forth. Practice verbal and nonverbal communication with each pass.

Build passing mechanics and partner communication in a low-pressure setting.

Skill 🔵 Color spots 🏀 Basketballs
🚶

Partner Passing with Movement

Partners 5 ft apart pass while moving together toward the basket. One partner shoots at the end of the run.

Transition from stationary to moving passes — introduces game-speed dynamics.

Skill Team 🏀 Basketballs 📍 Cones
✂️

Pass, Cut, and Shoot

Line at top of key, line on wing. Player 1 passes to Player 2, cuts to basket, receives return pass, and shoots. Coach acts as a guided defender.

Introduce basic basketball movement — the give-and-go concept in an adaptive format.

Skill Team 🏀 Basketballs 🔵 Floor spots
🎯

Passing into Target Nets

Teams line up and each player gets 3 attempts to pass the ball into a target net. Count successful passes per team.

Develop accuracy and passing strength in a structured, goal-oriented game.

Skill Fun 🏀 Basketballs 🔵 Spots 🥅 Target nets
⭕

Circle Passing

Players form a circle with one ball. Pass around the circle using different pass types — call the receiver's name before each pass.

Build passing variety, communication, and team cohesion in a group setting.

Team 🏀 Basketballs 📍 Cones
🌍

Around the World

Move spot-to-spot around the key and three-point line, shooting from each position before advancing to the next.

Build shooting confidence from multiple angles and distances.

Skill Fun 🔵 Color dots/cones 🏀 Basketballs
🔢

5-4-3-2-1 Shooting

Numbered spots 1–5 are placed around the basket. Players shoot in sequence, moving through each angle.

Encourage movement through multiple shooting angles with a clear, easy-to-follow structure.

Skill 🔵 Numbered spots 🏀 Basketballs
🏃

Layup & Shooting Lines

Two lines on each wing — one shoots or attempts a layup, the other rebounds. Players switch lines after each turn.

Develop layup mechanics and introduce rebounding roles in a flowing drill.

Skill Team 🏀 Basketballs 📍 Cones
⚡

Dribble, Pass, Shoot

Player dribbles from baseline through cones. A wing player cuts toward the basket. Dribbler passes to the cutter, who then shoots.

Combine dribbling, passing, cutting, and shooting in one connected sequence.

Skill Team 🏀 Basketballs 📍 Cones
🧊

Freeze Tag

Players dribble around the court with a partner or volunteer. If tagged by a coach, they're frozen. Partner unfreezes them by delivering a pass.

Keep dribbling skills active in a fun, social game that reinforces passing to help teammates.

Fun Team 🏀 Basketballs
🚦

Red Light, Green Light

Players dribble from baseline to baseline. Coach holds up a red or green sign — freeze on red, go on green. First to the far side wins.

Develop stop-and-start ball control and listening skills in an exciting race format.

Fun 🟢 Red/green sign 🏀 Basketballs
🦈

Sharks and Minnows

Players dribble from baseline to baseline trying to avoid the "shark" (coach). If tagged, that player becomes a shark and joins the coach.

Practice dribbling with spatial awareness under light pressure. High energy, high laughs.

Fun Game 🏀 Basketballs
⚡

Hustle Drill

Two players lie on their backs on either side of the key. Coach rolls a ball toward half-court and blows the whistle — both sprint to get it. First player gets it plays offense.

Develop explosive first-step reaction and build competitive fire in a safe, one-on-one format.
