Kickball

The most accessible program — kick, run, and celebrate every at-bat

Program Overview

Two groups, staggered start times — simple, immediate, and endlessly fun

🌱 Youngs & Intermediate

60-Minute Session

A quick warm-up and two focused drills, then straight into 40 minutes of scrimmage. The game is the point — drills are just a runway to get there.

  • Intro / Warm-up (5 min)
  • Fielding Bucket Drill (5 min)
  • Base Running (5 min)
  • Water Break (5 min)
  • Scrimmage (40 min)

Sessions start at 5:30 PM.

⭐ Advanced

60-Minute Session

Warm-up and water break, then the full session is scrimmage. For Advanced, the game IS the program — 50 minutes of pure kickball.

  • Intro / Warm-up (5 min)
  • Water Break (5 min)
  • Scrimmage (50 min)

Sessions start at 6:30 PM. Youngs finish just as Advanced arrives.

📅 Staggered Schedule: Kickball uses a unique staggered schedule — Youngs sessions run 5:30–6:30 PM and Advanced sessions run 6:30–7:30 PM. Youngs finish just as Advanced arrives, allowing staff to transition smoothly between groups.

Drills Library

Two quick drills that teach the core mechanics — then it's game time

🪣

Fielding Bucket Drill

Players line up in the infield. Coach rolls a kickball to each player one at a time. Player picks it up and places it in a bucket next to first base — that's the out mechanic. Requires 3 coaches: one managing the line, one managing the bucket, one rolling the ball.

Purpose: Fielding mechanics and understanding the out mechanic in a no-pressure environment.

Skill ⬛ bases 🪣 bucket 🔴 kickballs
💡 The bucket is a genius adaptation — it eliminates the throwing challenge of making an out and lets everyone successfully record an "out." Celebrate every successful bucket deposit!
🏃

Base Running

Players line up at home plate. Coaches and volunteers are stationed at each base (1st, 2nd, 3rd, home) to guide and cheer. Players are sent one at a time around all four bases.

Purpose: Learning base paths, scoring, and the structure of the game.

Skill Fun ⬛ bases
💡 Station the most enthusiastic volunteer at home plate to celebrate the "score" — high fives, cheers, the works. Make it feel like winning the championship every single time.

🔴 Scrimmage Rules

  • → All players take an at-bat before sides switch to field
  • → Coaches and volunteers position themselves in the field alongside participants
  • → The fielding bucket drill becomes the out mechanic during scrimmage — field it, bucket it, out!
  • → No strikeouts — every player gets to kick until they make contact
  • → Every runner who crosses home plate is celebrated — loudly and enthusiastically
