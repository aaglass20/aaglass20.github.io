Pickleball

Paddle skills, volleys, and serving — one of our fastest-growing programs

Program Overview

Two tiers, one court — adapted for every ability level

🌱 Youngs & Intermediate

Full Drill Session

Players spend the full 75-minute session rotating through structured drills with coach and volunteer support at every station.

  • Warm-up & Introduction (15 min)
  • Water Break
  • Drill 1 (12 min)
  • Drill 2 (12 min)
  • Water Break
  • Drill 3 (12 min)
  • Drill 4 (12 min)

Start with balloon drills and paddle work before introducing the actual ball and court.

⭐ Advanced

Drill + Scrimmage Session

Experienced players run 1–2 focused drills then move straight into a 40-minute scrimmage with peer volunteers on the court.

  • Warm-up (10 min)
  • 1–2 Drills (10 min each)
  • Water Break
  • Scrimmage (40 min)

1–2 ES players + 1–2 peers per team on court. Small court format.

Drills Library

All pickleball drills organized by category — click a tab to explore

🎈

Balloon Drills

Each player has a paddle and balloon. Coach calls different activities: keep balloon up with paddle, partner passing with balloon, don't let it touch the ground.

Purpose: Paddle-to-object contact in a low-pressure, slow-moving format.

Fun Skill 🎈 balloons 🏓 paddles
💡 Balloons are magic — they move slowly enough for everyone to track and react. Don't rush past this drill; it builds the paddle-confidence needed for everything else.
🏓

Paddle Work

Players bounce ball on paddle repeatedly (dribble it), balance it, or "walk the dog" — push ball along floor line while walking forward with the paddle.

Purpose: Paddle control and eye-hand coordination.

Skill Agility 🟡 pickleballs 🏓 paddles
🧱

Wall Ball

Players hit ball against wall with an optional target for focus. Work toward self-rallying — multiple consecutive hits against the wall.

Purpose: Striking mechanics and tracking.

Skill 🟡 pickleballs 🏓 paddles 🎯 optional target
🪣

Serving Into Buckets

Players on one side of court with 2–3 large buckets placed across the net. Take turns serving underhand into buckets.

Purpose: Serve mechanics and accuracy.

Skill Fun 🟡 pickleballs 🏓 paddles 🪣 buckets (2–3)
💡 Position buckets at different distances for different ability levels. Closer bucket = beginner. Far bucket = bonus challenge. Both count!
🤝

Partner Volley

Partners across from each other (or across net), volley back and forth. Start close, increase distance as confidence builds.

Purpose: Volley timing and tracking.

Skill Team 🟡 pickleballs 🏓 paddles
⚡

Reacting to Returns

Coach serves to each player on opposite side. Player must react and return ball over net. 2–3 serves per player before rotating.

Purpose: Real-game reaction and return mechanics.

Skill 🟡 pickleballs 🏓 paddles
🎯

Throwing to Targets

Players stand on kitchen line, throw pickleballs underhand at targets on opposite kitchen line.

Purpose: Build accuracy and court awareness without paddle pressure.

Skill Fun 🟡 pickleballs 🔴 color dots/targets

Scrimmage Format

1–2 ES players + 1–2 peer volunteers per team on court. Coaches play alongside and coach simultaneously. Rotate players frequently so everyone gets court time. Celebrate every point!

Equipment Checklist

Everything you need to run a pickleball session

🟡 Pickleballs (10–15)
🏓 Paddles (enough for all + extras)
🎈 Balloons (10+)
🪣 Buckets (2–3 large)
🔴 Color dots / targets
🥅 Portable net or existing court net
