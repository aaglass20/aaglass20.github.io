Volunteer Quick-Guide

😊 Patience First

Celebrate every small win loudly. A smile and "great job!" means the world.

🤲 Follow Their Lead

Let participants set the pace. Your job is to encourage, not to coach perfectly.

🗣️ Simple Language

Short, clear instructions. Demonstrate more than you explain.

🎉 Make It Fun

Energy is contagious. Bring enthusiasm and creativity every session.

Week 1 · August 22, 2026

⚽ Ball Familiarization & Passing

First session — focus on FUN and making participants feel welcome
Session Total
75 min
10:00
10 min

🌟 Welcome & Warm-Up

  • Greet every participant by name — high-fives all around
  • Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • Ball Roll Tag — everyone rolls the ball with their foot around a circle. No competition, just touch Fun
  • BDU players pair up 1:1 with each participant right away
Why we do this — tell your players: "We warm up so our bodies are ready to run and kick. It wakes us up and gets us excited to play!"
Coach tip: Set the tone — energy, smiles, and patience. Many participants may be meeting BDU players for the first time.
10:10
48 min · 4 × 12

🔄 Rotation Stations

Setup: Split into 4 groups. Each group spends 12 minutes at one station, then rotates. Every group hits every station.
🦶 Ball Touch Station
  • Sole Taps — tap the top of the ball alternating feet while stationary Skill
  • Side-to-Side Rolls — roll ball left and right with sole of foot
  • Toe-Ball-Toe — push ball forward with toe, pull back with sole, repeat
  • Tick Tocks — tap the ball back and forth between insides of both feet Skill
  • BDU players demo first, then guide their partner through each move
Why we do this — tell your players: "The more we touch the ball with our feet, the better our feet learn it. Lots of touches make soccer easier!"
Coach tip: Celebrate any ball contact as a win. For wheelchair participants, use hands or a ramp assist — any interaction counts.
🎯 Partner Passing
  • Close Passes — 3–4 feet apart, inside-of-foot passes. Focus on stopping the ball Skill
  • Step Back — after 3 successful passes, each pair takes one step back. How far can you go?
  • Encourage cheering between pairs — "Great pass!", "You've got this!"
Why we do this — tell your players: "Soccer is a team sport. Passing is how we share the ball and help our friends score!"
Coach tip: Adjust distance based on each participant's ability. Closer is always fine — the goal is connection, not distance.
🎵 Dribble to Music
  • Dribble on the Beat — dribble the ball around the space while music plays Fun
  • Freeze! — when the music stops, freeze your body AND stop the ball with your foot
  • Mix it up — slow songs = slow dribble, fast songs = quick feet
  • BDU players dribble too and freeze big and silly
Why we do this — tell your players: "Music makes soccer fun! Freezing helps us learn to stop the ball fast — like when a coach yells STOP!"
Coach tip: Have a speaker ready with 3–4 upbeat songs. Pause without warning so the freeze is a surprise.
🥅 Dribble, Pass, Score!
  • Player dribbles from a start cone to a middle cone Skill
  • At the middle cone, pass across to a BDU partner waiting on the side
  • Partner sets the ball — player runs onto it and shoots into a small goal Skill
  • Every player gets several turns — rotate quickly, cheer every shot
Why we do this — tell your players: "Real soccer is dribble, then pass, then shoot — all together! This is how goals happen in a real game."
Coach tip: Keep the goal close and the pass short. Narrate it: "Great dribble! Nice pass! What a shot!"
10:58
17 min

🏆 Small-Sided Games

Split by ability: Run both games simultaneously — Advanced group on one end, Beginners on the other.
⭐ Advanced — "Everybody Scores"
  • Pick a size based on numbers (3v3, 5v5, or 7v7). Small goals, no keeper; every player must score before the team can win Team
  • BDU players celebrate every goal (for either team!) with maximum energy
  • Rotate BDU players onto both teams to keep numbers even and energy high
  • Short games with quick restarts — keep it moving and upbeat
🦛 Beginners — Hungry Hungry Hippos
  • Two goals on each end. Pile all balls in the middle of the space Fun
  • Player picks a ball and does a skill first — BDU player calls one (toe taps, sole rolls, tick tocks) Skill
  • Then dribble or pass the ball into their goal — any style, any speed
  • Repeat until all balls are gone — each new ball gets a different skill called out
  • Count the goals out loud together — how many did we get?! Team
Coach tip: Keep the skill simple and change it each round. Lots of ball touches with a fun finish — not perfection!
Why we do this — tell your players: "Games are the fun part of soccer! We use everything we learned — touching, dribbling, passing, and scoring — and we cheer for our team!"

Week 2 · August 29, 2026

🌀 Dribbling & Ball Control

Move with the ball — creative, fun, and confidence-building
Session Total
75 min
10:00
10 min

🌟 Welcome Back & Warm-Up

  • Match up
  • Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • Dribble Parade — everyone dribbles freely in a big area, BDU players make it silly Fun
  • BDU players pair up 1:1 with each participant right away
Why we do this — tell your players: "Moving with the ball right away gets our feet warmed up and our brains in soccer mode. The more our feet touch the ball, the more natural it feels!"
Coach tip: Returning participants will be more comfortable. Build on those connections from Week 1.
10:10
48 min · 4 × 12

🔄 Rotation Stations

Setup: Split into 4 groups. Each group spends 12 minutes at one station, then rotates. Every group hits every station.
🌀 Cone Dribbling Course
  • Set up 3 courses side by side: wide weave (easy), tight weave (harder), hoop course (dribble in, stop inside hoop) Skill
  • BDU player demos first, then participant picks their course — choice builds confidence
  • Encourage moving up to the next course if they're ready
  • Cheer loudly every time they reach the end!
Why we do this — tell your players: "Dribbling through cones teaches our feet to control the ball while moving — the same skill real soccer players use to get past defenders!"
Coach tip: Set up all 3 courses before the session starts. Letting participants choose removes the fear of failure from the start.
🚦 Red Light / Green Light
  • Green Light — dribble forward Skill
  • Red Light — stop the ball with your sole (no hands)
  • Yellow Light — dribble in tiny circles
  • Blue Light (bonus BDU round) — dribble backwards
  • BDU players play too — make it a shared experience Team
Why we do this — tell your players: "Learning to stop the ball quickly is one of the most important skills in soccer. When we control where the ball goes AND when it stops, we control the game!"
Coach tip: Call colors loudly and with energy. Stopping is the key skill — give extra praise for clean stops.
🎵 Musical Dribble
  • Dribble freely around the space while music plays Fun
  • Freeze! — when the music stops, freeze your body AND stop the ball with your sole
  • Mix it up — slow songs = slow dribble, fast songs = quick feet
  • BDU players freeze big and silly — arms out, wide eyes — to model it
Why we do this — tell your players: "Dribbling to music helps us move freely with the ball without overthinking it. When the music stops and we freeze, we're practicing ball control without even realizing it!"
Coach tip: Have your phone/speaker ready with 3–4 songs. Pause without warning — the surprise freeze is half the fun!
🎯 Gate Goals
  • Set up 6–8 pairs of cones as mini gates scattered around the space Skill
  • Participants dribble through as many gates as possible in the time
  • BDU player counts aloud: "That's 3! Can we get 4?!" Fun
  • No wrong direction — any gate, any speed, any order counts
Why we do this — tell your players: "Dribbling through gates teaches us to look up and change direction with the ball — just like in a real game when we need to find open space!"
Coach tip: Adjust gate width per participant — wider for beginners, narrower for a challenge. Count together loudly so everyone hears the progress.

  • Pile all balls in the center — that's the treasure! Fun
  • Players dribble one ball at a time from the center pile to their team's "treasure chest" (goal or cone area) Skill
  • Return to the pile and get another — keep going until all treasure is collected
  • BDU player counts aloud and cheers every delivery: "That's 4 gold coins!" Team
Coach tip: No wrong speed, no wrong style — any dribble counts. Narrate every ball like it's the winning goal!
Why we do this — tell your players: "In a real game, everything comes together — dribbling, controlling, moving. This is our chance to use every skill we practiced today!"

Week 3 · September 5, 2026

🎯 Shooting & Finishing

Every player loves scoring a goal — make this one memorable
Session Total
75 min
10:00
10 min

🌟 Welcome & Warm-Up

  • High-energy greetings — BDU players are hype squad from the moment participants arrive
  • Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • Goal Parade — each player dribbles to the goal and shoots freely to warm up Fun
  • Kick-swings and ankle circles for shooting preparation
Why we do this — tell your players: "Shooting takes a confident leg and a focused mind. Warming up with the ball gets us ready to kick with purpose — and today, we're going to score a LOT!"
10:10
48 min · 2 × 24

🔄 Rotation Stations

Setup: Split into 2 groups. Each group spends 24 minutes at one station, then rotates. Both groups hit both stations.
🎯 Shooting Technique
  • Stationary Kicks — ball on the ground, practice laces-kick with BDU player holding the ball still Skill
  • Start 5 ft from goal, move back one step for every goal scored
  • BDU players celebrate EVERY shot, even misses — "So close! Try again!"
  • Target Shooting — use cones or discs to mark corners of the goal as targets Skill
Why we do this — tell your players: "Good shooting starts with good technique. When we practice kicking the right way, goals happen more often — and they feel amazing!"
Coach tip: For participants who can't kick, try hand-throw into the goal or a ramp. A goal is a goal — celebrate it all.
⚽ "Score a Goal" Challenge
  • Simple dribble → shoot sequence — BDU player sets the ball up, participant finishes Skill
  • Group celebrates every goal with a chant: "1… 2… 3… BDU!" Team
  • Goal Counter — keep a running team total on a whiteboard or cones. Can we hit 50 goals today? Fun
Why we do this — tell your players: "Every soccer player's dream is to score. When we do it over and over in practice, it stops feeling scary and starts feeling natural!"
Coach tip: Keep the line moving — short bursts, quick celebrations, next player up. Energy stays high when everyone is active.

17 min

  • BDU players celebrate every goal (for either team!) with maximum energy

  • Short games with quick restarts — keep it moving and upbeat
🤡 Beginners — Penalty Parade
  • Set up a wide, open goal close to the shooting line — easy distance, big target Fun
  • Each player takes turns shooting — BDU player sets the ball up for them Skill
  • BDU player is the "silly goalkeeper" — always dives the wrong way, always misses dramatically
  • Crowd goes WILD for every single goal — BDU players are the hype squad Team
  • Every player shoots multiple times — keep the line moving and the energy high
Coach tip: The sillier the keeper dive, the better. Every participant should feel like a superstar scorer!
Why we do this — tell your players: "Scoring in a game is the most exciting moment in soccer. All the shooting we practiced today leads to this — let it fly and celebrate every single one!"

Week 4 · September 12, 2026

🤝 Teamwork & Playing Together

Building bonds through the game — the best part of soccer
Session Total
75 min
10:00
10 min

🌟 Team Intro & Warm-Up

  • Form teams of 4 (2 BDU + 2 participants) — name your team Fun
  • Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • Juggle Relay — team sees how many total touches they can get (thigh, foot, head) without the ball dropping
  • Team cheer/chant — give each team 2 minutes to invent one
Why we do this — tell your players: "Teams that connect before the game connect better during it. Knowing your teammates' names and cheering for each other makes everything more fun!"
10:10
48 min · 2 × 24

🔄 Rotation Stations

Setup: Split into 2 groups. Each group spends 24 minutes at one station, then rotates. Both groups hit both stations.
🤝 Passing Combinations
  • Triangle Passing — 3-person triangles, pass and move to the open cone Skill
  • Call name before passing — "Sarah!" then kick
  • 1-2 Combo — pass to BDU player, get it back (wall pass), then shoot. Simple but empowering Skill
Why we do this — tell your players: "Passing is how we talk on the soccer field. When we pass to a teammate, we're saying 'I see you and I trust you!' — and that makes the whole team better."
Coach tip: Calling names builds connection. Participants light up when their name is called before a pass.
🎮 Team Challenges
  • Relay Race — dribble to cone and back, tag next teammate. Silly rules: weak foot only, hop, etc. Fun
  • Team Target Practice — team combines passes before shooting; each touch adds a multiplier to the goal Team
  • Encourage BDU players to cheer for other teams too — model good sportsmanship
Why we do this — tell your players: "Teamwork means working together to get something done. When we cheer each other on and celebrate together, we all get better — that's how teams win!"
10:58
17 min

🏆 Small-Sided Games

Split by ability: Run both games simultaneously — Advanced group on one end, Beginners on the other.
⭐ Advanced — "Everybody Scores"
  • Pick a size based on numbers (3v3, 5v5, or 7v7). Small goals, no keeper; every player must score before the team can win Team
  • BDU players celebrate every goal (for either team!) with maximum energy
  • Rotate BDU players onto both teams to keep numbers even and energy high
  • Short games with quick restarts — keep it moving and upbeat
🤝 Beginners — Buddy Ball
  • Pairs: one BDU player + one participant per pair Team
  • BDU player passes the ball gently to participant — participant runs onto it and shoots into the goal Skill
  • Switch roles — participant passes, BDU player finishes. Show it both ways!
  • Celebrate every score together as a pair — high fives, fist bumps, team cheer Fun
  • Gradually increase the pass distance as confidence builds
Coach tip: Frame the pass as a gift: "I'm passing to YOU because I trust you to score!" That connection is the whole point of Week 4.
Why we do this — tell your players: "Everything in soccer is more fun with teammates. When we pass, communicate, and celebrate together, that's what real soccer feels like!"
Coach tip: End with ALL players in a circle — each shares one word about their experience: "fun", "happy", "awesome"!

Week 5 · September 26, 2026 · FINAL SESSION

🏆 All-Star Celebration!

The grand finale — showcase everything they've learned and celebrate together
Session Total
75 min
🎉 Final Session! Go all-out on celebration, energy, and making each participant feel like a superstar. Consider bringing extra snacks or small tokens of appreciation.
10:00
10 min

🌟 Red Carpet Welcome

  • BDU players form a tunnel/line and cheer every participant in by name as they arrive
  • Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • Each participant gets a "All-Star" ribbon or sticker when they arrive Fun
  • Free-touch warm-up — just play freely and soak in the atmosphere
  • BDU players pair up 1:1 with each participant right away
Why we do this — tell your players: "We've been building skills all program. Today's warm-up celebrates everything we've worked for — every stretch and every touch leads to the big game!"
Coach tip: Start the energy at 11 from minute one. This is THEIR day.
10:10
55 min

🏆 All-Star Championship Game

  • Full-field game — ALL participants and ALL BDU volunteers together Team
  • Divide into 2 balanced teams, sprinkle BDU volunteers evenly across both — keep it competitive and fun
  • Rotate players every 10–12 minutes so everyone gets quality field time
  • BDU players on the sideline cheer loudly for both teams — no silent subs Fun
  • Celebrate every goal, every save, every great pass — ALL of it counts Team
Why we do this — tell your players: "This is what soccer is all about — playing together, competing with heart, and cheering for everyone on the field. Every person here earned their spot in this game!"
Coach tip: Keep the rotation fair and the energy high. The goal isn't a final score — it's that every participant walks off the field feeling like an All-Star.
11:05
10 min

🎗️ Closing Ceremony

  • Recognition for every participant — say each name aloud and have BDU players clap Team
  • Group photo opportunity — everyone together
  • BDU coaches/players share closing words about what the experience meant to them
  • Final cheer: "1… 2… 3… iCan!" → "1… 2… 3… BDU!"
Coach tip: End on the highest possible note. Send participants home feeling celebrated, seen, and loved by the BDU family.
