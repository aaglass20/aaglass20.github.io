Volunteer Quick-Guide

Keep these tips in mind every week — they make all the difference

😊

Patience First

Celebrate every small win loudly. A smile and "great job!" means the world.

🤲

Follow Their Lead

Let participants set the pace. Your job is to encourage, not to coach perfectly.

🗣️

Simple Language

Use short, clear instructions. Demonstrate more than you explain.

🎉

Make It Fun

Energy is contagious. Bring enthusiasm and creativity to every activity.

Weekly Session Plans

Week 1 · August 22, 2026

Ball Familiarization & Passing

First session — focus on FUN and making participants feel welcome!

⚽
10:00
🌟 Welcome & Warm-Up 10 min
  • 👋 Greet every participant by name — high-fives all around!
  • 🙆 Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • ⚽ Ball Roll Tag — everyone rolls the ball with their foot around a circle. No competition, just touch! Fun
  • 💡 BDU players pair up 1:1 with each participant right away
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "We warm up so our bodies are ready to run and kick. It wakes us up and gets us excited to play!"
💡 Set the tone: energy, smiles, and patience. Many participants may be meeting BDU players for the first time.
10:10
🔄 Rotation Stations 48 min · 4 × 12
Setup: Split into 4 groups. Each group spends 12 minutes at one station, then rotates. Every group hits every station by the end.
🦶 Ball Touch Station
  • ⚽ Sole Taps — tap the top of the ball alternating feet while stationary Skill
  • ↔️ Side-to-Side Rolls — roll ball left and right with sole of foot
  • 🔵 Toe-Ball-Toe — push ball forward with toe, pull back with sole, repeat in place
  • 🕰️ Tick Tocks — tap the ball back and forth between the insides of both feet, like a clock swinging Skill
  • 🌟 BDU players demo first, then guide their partner through each move
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "The more we touch the ball with our feet, the better our feet learn it. Lots of touches make soccer easier!"
💡 Celebrate any ball contact as a win. For wheelchair participants, use hands or a ramp assist — any interaction counts!
🎯 Partner Passing
  • 🟡 Close Passes — 3–4 feet apart, inside-of-foot passes. Focus on stopping the ball. Skill
  • 📏 Step Back — after 3 successful passes, each pair takes one step back. How far can you go?
  • 💬 Encourage cheering between pairs — "Great pass!", "You've got this!"
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Soccer is a team sport. Passing is how we share the ball with our friends and help them score!"
💡 Adjust distance based on each participant's ability. Closer is always fine — the goal is connection, not distance.
🎵 Dribble to Music
  • 🎶 Dribble on the Beat — dribble the ball around the space while music plays Fun
  • 🛑 Freeze! — when the music stops, freeze your body AND stop the ball with your foot
  • 🐢 Mix it up — slow songs = slow dribble, fast songs = quick feet
  • 🎉 BDU players dribble too and freeze big and silly
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Music makes soccer fun! Freezing helps us learn to stop the ball fast — like when a coach yells STOP!"
💡 Have your phone/speaker ready with 3–4 upbeat songs. Pause without warning so the freeze is a surprise every time.
🥅 Dribble, Pass, Score!
  • 🌀 Player dribbles from a start cone toward a middle cone Skill
  • 🤝 At the middle cone, pass across to a BDU partner waiting on the side
  • 🥅 Partner sets the ball up — player runs onto it and shoots into a small goal Skill
  • 🔁 Every player gets several turns — rotate quickly, cheer every shot
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Real soccer is dribble, then pass, then shoot — all together! This is how goals happen in a real game."
💡 Keep the goal close and the pass short. Every touch is a win — narrate it: "Great dribble! Nice pass! What a shot!"
10:58
🏆 Small-Sided Games 17 min
Split by ability: Run both games simultaneously — Advanced group on one end, Beginners on the other.
⭐ Advanced — "Everybody Scores"
  • 🔵 Pick a size based on numbers (3v3, 5v5, or 7v7). Small goals, no goalkeeper. Every player must score before the team can win Team
  • 🎉 BDU players celebrate every goal (for either team!) with maximum energy
  • 🤝 Rotate BDU players onto both teams to keep numbers even and energy high
  • ⏱️ Short games with quick restarts — keep it moving and upbeat
🦛 Beginners — Hungry Hungry Hippos
  • 🥅 Two goals on each end. Pile all balls in the middle of the space Fun
  • 🦶 Player picks a ball from the pile and does a skill on it first — BDU player calls one (toe taps, sole rolls, tick tocks) Skill
  • ⚽ Then dribble or pass the ball into their goal — any style, any speed
  • 🔁 Repeat until all balls are gone — each new ball gets a different skill called out
  • 🎉 Count the goals out loud together as a team — how many did we get?! Team
💡 Keep the skill simple and change it each round. The goal is lots of ball touches with a fun finish — not perfection!
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Games are the fun part of soccer! We use everything we learned — touching, dribbling, passing, and scoring — and we cheer for our team!"
Week 2 · August 29, 2026

Dribbling & Ball Control

Move with the ball — creative, fun, and confidence-building!

🌀
10:00
🌟 Welcome Back & Warm-Up 10 min
  • 👋 Match up
  • 🙆 Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • ⚽ Dribble Parade — everyone dribbles freely in a big area, BDU players make it silly Fun
  • 💡 BDU players pair up 1:1 with each participant right away
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Moving with the ball right away gets our feet warmed up and our brains in soccer mode. The more our feet touch the ball, the more natural it feels!"
💡 Returning participants will be more comfortable. Build on those connections from Week 1!
10:10
🔄 Rotation Stations 48 min · 4 × 12
Setup: Split into 4 groups. Each group spends 12 minutes at one station, then rotates. Every group hits every station by the end.
🌀 Cone Dribbling Course
  • 🔶 Set up 3 courses side by side: wide weave (easy), tight weave (harder), hoop course (dribble in, stop inside hoop) Skill
  • ⚽ BDU player demos first, then participant picks their course — choice builds confidence
  • ⬆️ Encourage moving up to the next course if they're ready
  • 🏅 Cheer loudly every time they reach the end!
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Dribbling through cones teaches our feet to control the ball while moving — the same skill real soccer players use to get past defenders!"
💡 Set up all 3 courses before the session starts. Letting participants choose removes the fear of failure from the start.
🚦 Red Light / Green Light
  • 🟢 Green Light — dribble forward! Skill
  • 🔴 Red Light — stop the ball with your sole (no hands)
  • 🟡 Yellow Light — dribble in tiny circles
  • 💙 Blue Light (bonus BDU round) — dribble backwards!
  • 🔵 Color Dot variant — scatter multi-colored spot markers around the station. Call a color ("Go to RED!") and everyone dribbles to a red dot. Rotate colors randomly. Skill
  • 🎉 BDU players play too — make it a shared experience Team
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Learning to stop the ball quickly is one of the most important skills in soccer. When we control where the ball goes AND when it stops, we control the game!"
💡 Call colors loudly and with energy. The stopping skill is key — give extra praise for clean stops. For the dot variant: keep dots close together so nobody has to travel far, and celebrate every arrival.
🥅 Goalie Introduction
  • 🔶 Cone Gap Warmup — line up 5 cones in a row with a gap between each. Participants stand at one end; BDU player rolls the ball slowly through each gap, one at a time Skill
  • 🙌 Participant scoops the ball up as it rolls through — any method that works: two hands, one hand, arms cradle. Seated and wheelchair participants can reach right in!
  • ⬆️ Once they have it, try the 3-step sequence: bounce it once → basket catch (arms wide, cradle it in) → full catch with both hands Skill
  • 🤝 Not feeling it? Totally fine — just pass it back to the BDU player. Any interaction with the ball counts!
  • 🥅 Light Saves — participant positions in front of the goal (standing, seated, or wheelchair) while BDU player rolls a slow, gentle ball toward the net Skill
  • 🛑 Stop it any way you can — foot, hands, body, just be there. Every stop is a SAVE! Celebrate it huge Team
  • 📈 Progression (if ready) — BDU volunteer takes a soft kick-shot toward goal. Announce "here it comes!" so there's no surprise Fun
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Every team needs a goalkeeper — the hero who protects the net! Scooping and catching builds the hands and bravery that goalkeepers use in every real game. When you make a save, the whole team wins!"
💡 Keep shots gentle, slow, and aimed right at the participant — say "it's coming to YOU!" first. Any stop counts: foot, hand, body block. For wheelchair participants, a hand stop or lean-in is a full save. Celebrate EVERY attempt like it's the World Cup.
🎯 Gate Goals → Shoot!
  • 🔵 Set up 6–8 pairs of cones as mini gates scattered near a real goal Skill
  • 🔢 Give each participant a number — "Your number is 3!" — that's how many gates they dribble through before they shoot Fun
  • ⚽ Participant dribbles through their gates in any order, any direction — BDU player counts aloud with them: "1… 2… 3…"
  • 🥅 As soon as they hit their number, BDU player calls "SHOOT!" — participant finishes on goal Skill
  • 🎉 Whole group erupts for every goal — BDU players are the full hype squad Team
  • ⬆️ Increase the number for confident participants — can you hit 5 gates before you shoot?
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Real soccer is all about moving with the ball, finding space, and then finishing. Gates teach us to dribble with a purpose — and the shot at the end makes it feel just like a real game!"
💡 Tune the number to each participant — 1 or 2 gates for beginners, 4–5 for confident dribblers. Keep the shot close to goal. BDU players count out loud so the whole space feels alive with energy.
10:58
🏆 Small-Sided Games 17 min
Split by ability: Run both games simultaneously — Advanced group on one end, Beginners on the other.
⭐ Advanced — "Everybody Scores"
  • 🔵 Pick a size based on numbers (3v3, 5v5, or 7v7). Small goals, no goalkeeper. Every player must score before the team can win Team
  • 🎉 BDU players celebrate every goal (for either team!) with maximum energy
  • 🤝 Rotate BDU players onto both teams to keep numbers even and energy high
  • ⏱️ Short games with quick restarts — keep it moving and upbeat
🏴‍☠️ Beginners — Treasure Hunt Dribble
  • 💰 Pile all balls in the center — that's the treasure! Fun
  • ⚽ Players dribble one ball at a time from the center pile to their team's "treasure chest" (goal or cone area) Skill
  • 🔁 Return to the pile and get another — keep going until all treasure is collected
  • 📢 BDU player counts aloud and cheers every delivery: "That's 4 gold coins!" Team
💡 No wrong speed, no wrong style — any dribble counts. Keep the energy up by narrating every single ball like it's the winning goal!
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "In a real game, everything comes together — dribbling, controlling, moving. This is our chance to use every skill we practiced today!"
Week 3 · September 5, 2026

Shooting & Finishing

Every player loves scoring a goal — make this one memorable!

🎯
10:00
🌟 Welcome & Warm-Up 10 min
  • 👋 High-energy greetings — BDU players are hype squad from the moment participants arrive!
  • 🙆 Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • ⚽ Goal Parade — each player dribbles to the goal and shoots freely to warm up Fun
  • 🙆 Kick-swings and ankle circles for shooting preparation
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Shooting takes a confident leg and a focused mind. Warming up with the ball gets us ready to kick with purpose — and today, we're going to score a LOT!"
10:10
🔄 Rotation Stations 48 min · 4 × 12
Setup: Split into 4 groups. Each group spends 12 minutes at one station, then rotates. Every group hits every station by the end.
🎯 Pass & Finish
  • 📍 BDU players spread out at 3–4 spots around the goal — left side, right side, straight ahead Skill
  • 🤝 BDU player passes the ball to the participant, who receives it and shoots first or second touch
  • 🔁 Rotate which BDU player serves — each angle is a new challenge
  • 🌟 Participants who need more time: BDU player sets the ball still so they can walk up and finish
  • 🎉 Celebrate every finish — the receive AND the shot Team
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "In a real game, almost every goal starts with a pass. When we receive the ball and shoot right away, we're doing exactly what happens in a real game!"
💡 Keep passes slow, on the ground, and aimed right at the participant's feet. Start with straight-on passes before adding angles. For wheelchair or low-mobility participants, have the BDU player place the ball one step in front of them — still counts as a receive!
🌀 Make Your Move
  • 🎩 BDU player demos all 4 moves first — slow and clear, one at a time. Participant picks their favorite Skill
  • 🦶 Dribble toward the standing BDU "defender," slow down, pull off the move — then shoot into the open goal! Skill
  • 🔁 Every player gets multiple turns — try a different move each round Fun
  • 🎉 Whole group cheers when someone pulls it off — "That's a REAL soccer move!" Team
The 4 Moves — BDU players demo each one:
  • 🔵 Rollover — put your sole on top of the ball and roll it sideways, then go the other way
  • 🟡 Feint — lean your whole body hard to one side like you're going that way, then push off and go the other way
  • 🔴 Outside Tap — tap the ball with the outside of your foot to knock it past the defender, then follow it
  • 🟢 Head Fake — look left, go right (or look right, go left!) — no touching the ball, just your eyes and shoulders
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Real soccer players use moves to get past defenders — and now you know four of them! Pick the one that feels right for YOU. That's what makes it YOUR move."
💡 The BDU "defender" should be completely passive — just standing still, maybe waving arms slowly. Never block or challenge — the goal is success every time. Let participants choose their move freely; ownership builds confidence. The head fake works great for wheelchair participants — no footwork needed, just eyes and shoulders.
🎳 Knock It Down
  • 🔶 Stack 5–6 foam cones or plastic bottles inside the goal mouth as "pins" Fun
  • ⚽ Each player gets 3 kicks — how many pins can they knock over? Skill
  • 🎉 BDU player counts and celebrates every pin out loud — "That's 4! That's a STRIKE!" Team
  • 🔁 Reset fast and rotate — every player gets multiple rounds
  • 🌟 Try to beat your own record each round — personal best is the goal
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Real shooting is about aiming — hitting what you're looking at. When we knock those pins down, our brain is learning to aim the ball exactly where we want it!"
💡 Place pins close together and close to the shooter — big satisfying crash on contact. For wheelchair or low-mobility participants, roll or push the ball along the ground, any contact counts. Foam pins are safer and easier to reset than anything rigid.
🌈 Call Your Shot
  • 🎨 Divide the goal into 3 zones using colored pinnies or cones: Left (🔵), Middle (🟡), Right (🔴) Skill
  • 📢 BDU player calls a color — "BLUE!" — participant aims for that zone and shoots
  • 🥅 Any ball that lands in the right zone is a WIN — distance and power don't matter Skill
  • 🎉 Hit the zone? Everyone celebrates — "Called it AND scored!" Team
  • ⬆️ Ready for more? Participant calls their OWN color before kicking Fun
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "In a real game, a good shooter picks a spot BEFORE they kick — they don't just blast it and hope. Calling your shot is what makes the difference!"
💡 Keep the goal close and zones wide — success rate matters more than difficulty. If a participant struggles to aim, have the BDU player point physically at the target zone and say "kick it there!" Visual + verbal cues together work best for this audience.
10:58
🏆 Small-Sided Games 17 min
Split by ability: Run both games simultaneously — Advanced group on one end, Beginners on the other.
⭐ Advanced — "Everybody Scores"
  • 🔵 Pick a size based on numbers (3v3, 5v5, or 7v7). Small goals, no goalkeeper. Every player must score before the team can win Team
  • 🎉 BDU players celebrate every goal (for either team!) with maximum energy
  • 🤝 Rotate BDU players onto both teams to keep numbers even and energy high
  • ⏱️ Short games with quick restarts — keep it moving and upbeat
🔫 Beginners — Ready, Aim, Fire!
  • 👥 Split into 4 teams — each team forms a line behind their ball, equal distance from goal Team
  • 📢 Director calls it out loud and slow: "READY…" — everyone gets set. "AIM…" — eyes on goal. "FIRE!" — all four shoot at the exact same time Fun
  • 🧤 One BDU volunteer is the "goalkeeper" — they face the entire firing squad alone. They CANNOT save them all and they know it — huge dramatic reactions on every goal Fun
  • 🏆 Keep score by team — each goal counts as a point for that team. Run 5–6 rounds, then crown a champion Team
  • 🔁 Next player in each line steps up after every round — quick rotation, everyone fires multiple times
  • 🎉 BDU players on the sideline go WILD for every goal — the louder, the better Team
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "This is the firing squad — and the goalkeeper has NO chance! All that shooting practice we did today? This is where you let it ALL go. Ready… Aim… FIRE!"
💡 The keeper's job is pure entertainment — dive early, miss dramatically, look horrified as every ball goes in. The score is real but the losing keeper is the star of the show. Keep the "Ready, Aim, Fire!" call slow and dramatic — build the tension before every volley. Set balls up for participants who need it before calling "Ready."
Week 4 · September 12, 2026

Teamwork & Playing Together

Building bonds through the game — the best part of soccer!

🤝
10:00
🌟 Team Intro & Warm-Up 10 min
  • 👥 Form teams of 4 (2 BDU + 2 participants) — name your team! Fun
  • 🙆 Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • ⚽ Juggle Relay — team sees how many total touches they can get (thigh, foot, head) without the ball dropping
  • 💪 Team cheer/chant — give each team 2 minutes to invent one
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Teams that connect before the game connect better during it. Knowing your teammates' names and cheering for each other makes everything more fun!"
10:10
🔄 Rotation Stations 48 min · 4 × 12
Setup: Split into 4 groups. Each group spends 12 minutes at one station, then rotates. Every group hits every station by the end.
🤝 Passing Combinations
  • 🔁 Triangle Passing — 3-person triangles, pass and move to the open cone Skill
  • 📢 Call name before passing — "Sarah!" then kick
  • 💛 1-2 Combo — pass to BDU player, get it back (wall pass), then shoot. Simple but empowering! Skill
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Passing is how we talk on the soccer field. When we pass to a teammate, we're saying 'I see you and I trust you!' — and that makes the whole team better."
💡 Calling names builds connection. Participants will light up when their name is called before a pass!
🎮 Team Challenges
  • 🏁 Relay Race — dribble to cone and back, tag next teammate. Silly rules: dribble with weak foot only, hop, etc. Fun
  • 🎯 Team Target Practice — team combines passes before shooting, each touch adds a multiplier to the goal Team
  • 📸 Encourage BDU players to cheer for other teams too — model good sportsmanship
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Teamwork means working together to get something done. When we cheer each other on and celebrate together, we all get better — that's how teams win!"
🏗️ Build-a-Goal
  • 📏 Simple rule: the team must complete 3 passes before anyone can shoot — and every touch must go to a different person Team
  • 🤝 BDU players count the passes out loud as a group: "One… two… THREE — SHOOT!" Skill
  • 🥅 When the shot goes in, the whole team scored that goal — not just the shooter Team
  • ⬆️ Feeling it? Bump to 4 or 5 passes before the shot — how connected can the team get? Fun
  • 🎉 Celebrate every successful pass chain AND every goal — both are wins
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "In real soccer, the best goals are built by the whole team — pass by pass. When everyone touches the ball before the shot, everyone owns the goal!"
💡 Keep the passing area small and the goal close — this isn't about athleticism, it's about connection. If a pass chain breaks, just reset with no pressure and try again. BDU players counting aloud keeps the energy up and makes every step feel like progress.
📣 Calling All Teammates
  • ⚽ Three balls in play at once across the team — it's controlled chaos! Fun
  • 📢 The rule: you can only receive a pass if you communicate for the ball first — voice, wave, gesture, clap, point, tap a shoulder — any signal that says "I'm here, pass it to me!" Team
  • 🌟 BDU players model it first — shout it, wave it, sign it — so everyone sees there's no one right way to ask Fun
  • 🎯 The clearest communicator gets the ball — every style of asking counts equally Skill
  • 🎉 Celebrate every signal just as much as every goal — the communication IS the skill here Team
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Great teammates communicate with each other! In real soccer, players are always letting each other know they're open. Your voice, your hands, your signal — whatever works for YOU — is how your teammates know you're there!"
💡 Any form of communication counts — voice, gesture, wave, noise, raised hand, eye contact, tapping someone's arm. BDU players should model a variety of signals first so participants see that no single style is "right." Celebrate every form of asking equally — the goal is connection, not volume.
10:58
🏆 Small-Sided Games 17 min
Split by ability: Run both games simultaneously — Advanced group on one end, Beginners on the other.
⭐ Advanced — "Everybody Scores"
  • 🔵 Pick a size based on numbers (3v3, 5v5, or 7v7). Small goals, no goalkeeper. Every player must score before the team can win Team
  • 🎉 BDU players celebrate every goal (for either team!) with maximum energy
  • 🤝 Rotate BDU players onto both teams to keep numbers even and energy high
  • ⏱️ Short games with quick restarts — keep it moving and upbeat
🎊 Beginners — 100 Goals Together
  • 🔢 Write 100 on a whiteboard or call it out loud — that's the team's goal for the whole group Team
  • ⚽ Every goal scored by anyone in the group counts toward the total — BDU players narrate the running tally like a countdown: "That's 47! Only 53 more!" Fun
  • 🚀 Keep balls flowing fast — the moment one goes in, the next player is already shooting. No waiting, no turns, just goals Fun
  • 📣 Every 10 goals, BDU players lead a big group cheer — energy builds as the number climbs Team
  • 🏆 When you hit 100, the whole group celebrates like a championship — it's EVERYONE'S record Team
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "This isn't about one person scoring — it's about ALL of us scoring together. Every single goal you make right now counts for the whole team. Let's get to 100!"
💡 Set balls up fast and keep the energy moving — the countdown is the engine. If the group is small, start at 50 instead of 100. BDU players celebrate each milestone (25, 50, 75) with escalating reactions so excitement builds toward 100. Every participant's goal counts equally — that's the whole point.
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "Everything in soccer is more fun with teammates. When we pass, communicate, and celebrate together, that's what real soccer feels like!"
💡 End with ALL players in a circle — each shares one word about their experience: "fun", "happy", "awesome"!
Week 5 · September 26, 2026 · FINAL SESSION

All-Star Celebration!

The grand finale — showcase everything they've learned and celebrate together!

🏆
🎉
Final Session! This is the culmination of the whole program. Go all-out on celebration, energy, and making each participant feel like a superstar. Consider bringing extra snacks or small tokens of appreciation!
10:00
🌟 Red Carpet Welcome 10 min
  • 🎺 BDU players form a tunnel/line and cheer every participant in by name as they arrive
  • 🙆 Seated or standing stretches with the ball
  • 🏅 Each participant gets an "All-Star" ribbon or sticker when they arrive Fun
  • ⚽ Free-touch warm-up — just play freely and soak in the atmosphere
  • 💡 BDU players pair up 1:1 with each participant right away
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "We've been building skills all program. Today's warm-up celebrates everything we've worked for — every stretch and every touch leads to the big game!"
💡 Start the energy at 11 from minute one. This is THEIR day!
10:10
🏆 All-Star Championship Game 55 min
  • 🏟️ Full-field game — ALL participants and ALL BDU volunteers together Team
  • ⚖️ Divide into 2 balanced teams, sprinkle BDU volunteers evenly across both — keep it competitive and fun
  • 🔄 Rotate players every 10–12 minutes so everyone gets quality field time
  • 🌟 BDU players on the sideline cheer loudly for both teams — no silent subs Fun
  • 🎉 Celebrate every goal, every save, every great pass — ALL of it counts Team
🤔
Why we do this — tell your players: "This is what soccer is all about — playing together, competing with heart, and cheering for everyone on the field. Every person here earned their spot in this game!"
💡 Keep the rotation fair and the energy high. The goal isn't a final score — it's that every participant walks off the field feeling like an All-Star.
11:05
🎗️ Closing Ceremony 10 min
  • 🏅 Recognition for every participant — say each name aloud and have BDU players clap Team
  • 💛 Group photo opportunity — everyone together!
  • 🤝 BDU coaches/players share closing words about what the experience meant to them
  • 💙 Final cheer: "1… 2… 3… iCan!" → "1… 2… 3… BDU!"
💡 End on the highest possible note. Send participants home feeling celebrated, seen, and loved by the BDU family.
