⚡ How to use FuelUp
↔
Drag & Drop
Drag any item from the left palette onto a time slot on your timeline.
👆
Tap to Add
On mobile, tap a palette item to select it (it highlights green), then tap any time slot to place it.
🔍
Search by Category
Type fruit, protein, dairy, veggie, treat, pregame, recovery, or breakfast to filter all items at once.
🥗
Sides
Drop a Side onto the same time slot as a meal to balance it — adds fruit, veg, dairy, or protein without counting as a snack.
♥
Favorites
Hover any palette item and click ♥ to pin it to the top of the list so your go-to foods are always first.
📋
Copy Day
Click Copy Day in the top bar to copy your full plan to one or more other days this week.
🌅
Breakfast for Dinner
Breakfast foods work at any meal time! The app figures out if it's breakfast, lunch, or dinner by when you place it — not what it is.
⚽
Game Day Rules
Add a sport event (game, meet, match) to unlock extra checks: pre-game meal timing, light pre-game snack, and halftime fuel.
🥗
Food Groups
The right panel tracks your daily USDA MyPlate progress — grains, veggies, fruit, dairy, and protein — with live progress bars.
🌟
Green Day
Pass all Day Check rules and the status dot turns green. Check the dot on each day in the week bar to see your whole week at a glance.
